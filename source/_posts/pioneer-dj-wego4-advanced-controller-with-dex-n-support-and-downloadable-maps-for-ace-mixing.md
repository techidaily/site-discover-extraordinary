### Key Pioneer DJ DDJ-WeGO4 Features

* Easy to connect
* Built-in soundcard
* Customizable EQs
* Professional features including hot cues, samplers, pad FX, and loops

### Specifications:

* Soundcard: 24 bit/48 kHz
* Frequency range: 20 to 20,000 Hz
* Channels: 2
* Deck control: 2
* Jog wheel: 109 mm
* Sampler: 4 per deck
* Hot cues: 4 per deck
* Inputs: (1) mic 1/4″ TS jack
* Outputs: (1) master RCA, (2) headphone monitor (1/4″ jack & mini-jack)
* USB: 1 USB B port + 1 USB A port
* Dimensions: 380mm X 59mm X 240mm (W x H x D)
* Wieght: 1.8kg
